# Human Evaluation Rubric · Juno

_Module 5. Generated by `M5 - Human Evaluation Rubric.html`. Paste over `05-evals/human-rubric.md`._

## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno's capabiity to synthesize customer feedback and prioritize it based on frequency of similar requests and company goals.
- **Value proposition:** I need to be able to have a prioritized backlog in Jira with an insight on what to work on next

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Source accuracy | Was Juno able to state the source and back its findings? | 1 to 5 | No, no sources were stated. | Yes, but not for all findings  | Yes, stated the source in a format that was easy to read. |
| Failure tracking (deal-breaker) | When a failure occurred, did Juno send you a list of failures in a Google doc and send an email to team@gmail.com  | Yes / No | No email was sent  | n/a | An email was sent with a google doc to team@gmail.com with clear listing of the failures |
| Prioritization (deal-breaker) | Does Juno prioritize feedback correctly? | Yes / No | Prioritizes feedback but misses important patterns. | n/a | Prioritizes feedback considering customer frequency and company goals.  |
| Actionable | Does Juno create Jira tickets that are actionable | Yes / No | Created tickets are unclear and vague. | n/a | Can be picked up by engineers, the ticket is well defined and detailed. |
| Latency | Did you receive an output within 30 seconds? | Yes / No | No, it was delayed and it took more than 30 seconds. | n/a | Yes the tickets were prioritized within 30 seconds of my request |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** 15 answers every week
- **Who scores them with you:** Engineering manager and another product manager
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** We will get into a discussion on the why and choose scores that fit the goal more accurately or involve another person.

## Pass bar

An average of 90% yes or a score of 3-5.

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Failure tracking: any "no"
- Prioritization: any "no"
- randomly prioritized tickets without a source or patterns.
