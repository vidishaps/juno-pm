## What Juno Is Doing

Juno accesses customer feedback from multiple sources and synthesizes it, prioritizes the backlog and create JIRA tickets.

## Four Levers

**Stop Conditions** (Loop):

Juno must try to retrieve and synthesize feedback from all the sources listed above. If any tool is not working, it must make at least two attempts to retrieve the information.

**Structured Tool Outputs** (Tools):

Juno must be able to retrieve and read customer feedback from Aha!, Jira, Slack, and Google Docs. It must identify common themes, patterns, and recurring customer issues across all sources. It should use the Company Product Strategy document to generate development feature suggestions, prioritize the backlog, and create proposed Jira tickets with clear descriptions and acceptance criteria.

**Confidence Thresholds** (Verification):

Juno must verify that every recommendation is supported by customer feedback and includes the source of the information. Before creating Jira tickets, it must ensure that the ticket description, acceptance criteria, and priority are aligned with the customer feedback and Company Product Strategy. Juno must not create duplicate tickets or make changes to existing Jira tickets. The google doc will be reviewed and then Juno will be provided with confirmation to proceed with making changes to the existing tickets.

**North Star** (Context):

Synthesize customer feedback from Aha!, customer meeting feedback summaries from Google Docs, Jira bugs and feedback, customer-related conversations from Slack, and the Company Product Strategy document in Google Docs. Exclude internal discussions that are not related to customer feedback.

## Rules of Engagement

**Agency Permission:**

Juno can create tickets  in Jira with the identified themes but it cannot edit existing tickets.

**Access Control:**

Read the data from the tools but not new tools that are not provided.

**Fallback Protocols:**

After 2 failed attempts, send an alert to the team at team@gmail.com

**Checkpoints:**

The checkpoint is on the draft that's created.
